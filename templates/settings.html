Hard limits for {{ env }}

These limits are stored separately for DEMO and LIVE. The AI sees them in its prompt, and the engine applies them after the decision, so a suggestion cannot override a filled-in limit. Leave a field blank and that limit is off — the AI decides sizing and cash.

  • Max position % — any holding above this share of the portfolio is cut. The excess weight moves to cash.
  • Min cash % — if cash would sit below this floor, the other holdings are reduced in proportion until cash meets it.
  • Max trades / day — counts orders already placed today. Once the cap is reached, later orders in the cycle are blocked.
  • Max order amount — an order larger than this is scaled down to the cap. If the scaled size is too small to place, the order is skipped.

Drawdown protection

Drawdown is the drop from the highest trading equity seen so far: (peak − current) / peak. Deposits and withdrawals are taken out of that equity, so adding or removing cash does not by itself move the peak. Each level is optional — leave it blank to disable it. Hard stop must be higher than the safe alert.

  • Safe alert — when drawdown reaches this %, risk is forced to low, minimum cash rises to the safe-mode cash floor (50% if that field is blank), and the AI is told to trade defensively. The autopilot keeps running. Safe mode clears on its own once drawdown falls below 70% of the threshold, and the risk level from before protection is restored.
  • Hard stop — when drawdown reaches this %, the autopilot stops and risk is set to low. It stays stopped until you clear it. A market recovery does not restart it.
  • Unlock — Clear protection, or changing risk on the dashboard, turns protection off. It stays off until drawdown eases below 70% of the active threshold. After that, protection can trigger again.
